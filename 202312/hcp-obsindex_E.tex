Table \ref{tab:obsindex} shows the index values (medians) for each class.
Among highly cited papers, Class G (Growing) is the most prevalent, followed by Class Nm and Class O, with Classes FP and SB appearing at a similar frequency.
It is worth noting that Class FP (Flash in the pan) papers tend to be older, as classification into this category requires not only high citations early in their lifetime but also a sufficiently long period of low citations afterward.
Table \ref{tab:mcpobsindex} presents the index values for the most-cited papers.
The ranges of the index values for the most-cited papers are wide, suggesting that they do not necessarily originate from a specific class.
Notably, two of them are classified into Class O; however, an examination of their citation curves reveals clear multi-peakedness, confirming them as revival papers.
